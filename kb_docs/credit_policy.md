# SME Working-Capital Credit Policy (v3.2, synthetic)

## 1. Eligibility
1.1 Business vintage must be at least 2 years.
1.2 Bureau score of 650 or above is required for standard approval; 600-649 requires underwriter review; below 600 is a decline unless an exception is approved by the Credit Head.
1.3 No more than one delinquency (30+ DPD) in the last 24 months for standard approval.

## 2. Repayment capacity
2.1 Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR proxy = average monthly net cash flow / proposed monthly payment) must be at least 1.25 for approval.
2.2 DSCR between 1.00 and 1.25 requires underwriter review with documented mitigants.
2.3 More than 3 negative-net-cash-flow months in a 12-month window is a HIGH-risk indicator.

## 3. Ticket size and authority
3.1 Straight-through approval authority: up to 250,000 for LOW-risk applicants only.
3.2 All declines must be reviewed by a human underwriter (adverse-action fairness requirement).
3.3 Tickets above 1,000,000 are outside the agent's authority and must be approved at branch credit committee.

## 4. Documentation
4.1 Every recommendation must cite the policy clauses relied upon and the bureau reference.
